Premium varieties like Honeycrisp (\$3.18 loss, 1.5\% juice routing) and Jazz (\$0 loss, 0\% juice routing) maintain value through accurate routing. The total \$186.41 loss across 133 juice-routed samples demonstrates the economic significance of routing accuracy. Loss calculations based on market prices: premium \$2.80/lb, standard \$1.75/lb, juice \$0.06/lb.}
